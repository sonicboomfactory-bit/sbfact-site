01. Publisher and scope

BrickArrow is a free Android game published under the name SBFact / Sonic Boom Factory. This policy covers the game and, separately, this website.

Data enquiries: sonicboomfactory@gmail.com. SBFact is your contact for processing it determines; providers may also process data under their own responsibility, according to their terms.

02. Data and purposes

BrickArrow uses Google AdMob / Google Mobile Ads for advertising and Google User Messaging Platform (UMP) for privacy choices. Advertising processing depends on the SDK version, configuration and your choices.

Advertising · Google AdMob / Mobile Ads

The SDK may collect and transmit to Google an IP address, which can be used to estimate approximate location, interactions (launches, taps and views), performance diagnostics and identifiers, including the Android advertising ID and app set ID. These are used to serve and measure ads, for analytics and to prevent fraud. Location estimated from an IP address is not a reading of your GPS location.

Interstitial ads are also present. Rewarded ads offer a reward when you choose to watch an ad and meet its conditions. Choosing whether to watch does not guarantee that no technical processing occurs beforehand.

Privacy choices · Google UMP

UMP checks the status of advertising choices and presents applicable messages, notably in the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom and Switzerland. Consent information is used to apply your choices; this service does not analyse your gameplay progression.

Local data

Your scores, progression and settings are saved locally. Game data and settings stored only on your device are not transmitted merely by being saved locally. Local storage is separate from advertising SDK processing. Clearing BrickArrow’s app data in Android settings removes its local saves; this does not erase data already transmitted to Google.

GameAnalytics disabled in the Release

In the Android Release version covered by this policy, GameAnalytics is disabled and does not collect or send events in this version.

03. Consent and control

Where consent is required, you may accept, refuse or manage the options offered in the game’s privacy form. Advertising processing subject to consent requires valid consent before activation.

To change or withdraw an advertising choice, use the game’s privacy options entry when available or required. Contact SBFact if you cannot find it. This website cannot change choices stored on your phone.

Depending on your choices and Google’s configuration, limited or non-personalised ads, or no ads, may be served. Refusing personalisation does not necessarily stop all technical data processing. Depending on your Android version, system settings also let you delete or reset your advertising identifier; this does not withdraw every consent or erase data already processed.

Withdrawal applies going forward and does not affect earlier lawful processing. Technical security and fraud prevention may rely on other legal bases, including legitimate interests, within applicable law. Rights requests and legal obligations are handled in accordance with applicable law.

04. Providers and transfers

Data is transmitted to Google for advertising and privacy choices. SBFact may view advertising reports provided by AdMob. No events are sent to GameAnalytics in the Release described here. Subprocessors or competent authorities may be involved where necessary to provide the service or required by law.

These services may process data in countries other than yours, including outside the EEA. Relevant transfers must use safeguards required by applicable law, such as standard contractual clauses where appropriate. Provider terms describe their roles, recipients and safeguards.

05. Retention and security

Local data remains on the device until erased or replaced by the game. Any restoration depends on Android backup settings. Google’s retention depends on the data type, purpose and service rules; this policy does not promise a single retention period or immediate deletion of all advertising data.

Google states that data collected by Google Mobile Ads is encrypted in transit using TLS. This protects transmission and does not mean no data is collected. No measure guarantees absolute security.

Clearing local data, uninstalling the game or deleting the Android advertising ID does not automatically erase data held by Google. To request deletion, contact sonicboomfactory@gmail.com and specify BrickArrow. SBFact handles requests concerning data under its control and explains applicable steps with Google, which manages data under its own responsibility. Some data may be retained to meet legal obligations or prevent abuse.

06. Your rights and minors

Depending on your circumstances and applicable law, you may request access, correction, deletion, restriction or portability, object to certain processing and withdraw consent. Contact SBFact with the game name and your request. Do not send passwords; only information needed to identify the request should be requested.

You may lodge a complaint with your competent data protection authority, including the CNIL in France.

If you are a parent or legal guardian and believe a child’s data has been processed without required authorisation, contact SBFact. Available information does not establish a target age range or a specific child-directed configuration.

07. This website’s privacy

This website’s files add no trackers, advertising SDKs, analytics tools, cookies or local storage. Fonts and illustrations are local. No form sends data.

The hosting provider may process technical connection information (such as IP address, time and request) to deliver and secure pages, under its own rules. External links lead to sites with their own policies. If you email us, your message and email address are used to handle your enquiry.

08. Further information

This policy will be updated if the game’s practices change. The displayed date indicates the latest revision.
